REPLIT AGENT PROMPT — Autonomous Daily Prayer Poster for PrayForChange.org

Paste everything below this line into Replit Agent.

====================================================================

Build me a self-running server app that posts one daily crisis prayer to
PrayForChange.org automatically, with no dependency on my local machine, my
browser, or me being logged in anywhere. It must run entirely on Replit on a
daily schedule and finish on its own.

GOAL
Once per day the app should: find the most pressing global humanitarian /
crisis news story from a fixed list of approved news sources, draft a short
interfaith prayer about it, avoid repeating any story it has covered in the
last 14 days, attach a real news photo, publish it to prayforchange.org, and
email me a confirmation. If publishing fails, it should email me the full
drafted prayer so I can post it manually.

ARCHITECTURE / STACK
- Python.
- Scheduling: use Replit's scheduled-deployment (cron) capability to run the
  job once per day at 07:00 Europe/Rome. The job should run to completion and
  exit — do NOT build an always-on server. (Make the run time easy to change.)
- Persistence: a small SQLite file (or Replit DB) to store the history of
  posted stories so duplicates can be detected across runs.
- LLM: call the Anthropic API (Claude) for story selection and prayer drafting.
- Posting: see POSTING below — prefer an API, fall back to headless Playwright.
- All credentials live in Replit Secrets, never in code.

SECRETS THE APP NEEDS (set these in the Secrets tab)
- ANTHROPIC_API_KEY
- PFC_EMAIL + PFC_PASSWORD   (the app logs in to the website itself — no API)
- NOTIFY_EMAIL_TO        (where confirmations go)
- SMTP_HOST / SMTP_USER / SMTP_PASS / SMTP_PORT   (for sending the email; a
  Gmail app password is fine, or use a transactional email service)

THE DAILY JOB — run these steps in order:

STEP 1 — FIND TODAY'S STORY (approved sources ONLY, no web search)
Do NOT use any web-search API. Fetch directly from these approved domains only:
reuters.com, apnews.com, france24.com, cnn.com, nytimes.com,
washingtonpost.com, npr.org, cbsnews.com, abcnews.go.com, bbc.com,
theguardian.com, news.sky.com, channelnewsasia.com, abc.net.au, smh.com.au,
aljazeera.com, news.un.org, who.int, unicef.org, dw.com.

Fetch the world/news section pages of at least 3 of these (use RSS feeds where
the outlet offers them, otherwise fetch and parse the section HTML). Start with
Reuters world, AP world news, and BBC world. Extract the current headlines and
article links. Pass the collected headlines to Claude and ask it to pick the
single most pressing global crisis or humanitarian story, prioritising:
natural disasters, humanitarian crises (outbreaks, famine, refugees), mass
casualty events, health and climate emergencies.

AVOID politically partisan stories, elections, political scandals, culture-war
topics, and celebrity news — the platform must stay neutral and welcoming to
all faiths and political views. If nothing major is breaking, pick a NEW
development in an ongoing crisis (a fresh angle / latest update), not a generic
repeat. The only exception for revisiting a war is major peace talks.

STEP 2 — MULTI-MARKET VALIDATION (record the tier)
Confirm the chosen story appears on the approved sites across markets:
- Tier 1 (preferred): appears in at least 3 of these 4 groups — Wire (Reuters,
  AP), US (CNN, NYT, WaPo, NPR, CBS, ABC), UK (BBC, Guardian, Sky),
  Asia-Pacific (CNA, ABC AU, SMH). Prefer Tier 1.
- Tier 2 (allowed): on at least 2 wire services or UN agencies.
- Tier 3 (skip): only one outlet.
Verify by fetching the relevant approved sites. Store which outlets confirmed it.

STEP 3 — DE-DUPLICATE
Load the last 14 days of posted stories from the database. If today's candidate
is the same topic as anything in that window, discard it and go back to the next
best candidate from Step 1. (Store a canonical topic tag per story, e.g.
"sudan-famine", and compare on that, not just the headline.)

STEP 4 — DRAFT THE PRAYER (Claude)
TITLE: starts with "Pray for...", under 60 characters.
STORY: exactly 3 paragraphs, 100-150 words total.
  - Para 1: what happened — specific facts, numbers, location, scale.
  - Para 2: why it matters — human impact, one or two sentences.
  - Para 3: the prayer — name the people affected (families, health workers,
    volunteers, communities); end with hope.
STYLE: dignified not dramatic; specific not abstract; interfaith — must work for
Christians, Muslims, Jews, Hindus, Buddhists and secular-spiritual readers, so
use "we pray", never "Dear Lord" or tradition-specific language; never take a
political side; lead with facts, not emotion.

STEP 5 — GET A REAL NEWS PHOTO
Fetch the chosen article from Reuters or AP (server-side fetch has no CORS
problem) and extract the main article image URL, then download the image bytes
for upload. If that article has no usable image, try the same story on BBC or
CNN. Never use an AI-generated image.

  >> LICENSING NOTE FOR JACK (read once, then decide):
  Auto-downloading and republishing wire-service photos every day is a real
  copyright exposure on a public site. Safer options if you'd rather not risk
  it: (1) use outlets/agencies that publish freely-licensed imagery, (2) pull
  from a licensed stock/Creative-Commons source, (3) take a proper wire licence,
  or (4) allow the platform's own image generation. Tell me which and I'll
  swap this step. Leaving the Reuters/AP behaviour in for now as in the original.

STEP 6 — PUBLISH (see POSTING below)

STEP 7 — VERIFY & RECORD
Confirm the prayer is live (capture the published page URL and a screenshot).
Save to the database: date, title, canonical topic tag, source links, tier,
published URL.

STEP 8 — NOTIFY ME (email to NOTIFY_EMAIL_TO)
Send an email containing:
- the news source(s) with links (approved list only)
- the prayer title and the link to the published page
- the tier (1 or 2) and which approved outlets confirmed the story
- the embed code:
  <iframe src="https://prayforchange.org/embed/[slug]" width="100%" height="200" frameborder="0"></iframe>
- attach or link the screenshot of the live page

POSTING (no API — fully self-contained on Replit)
Use headless Playwright (Chromium, installed and running on Replit's own
servers — nothing runs on my computer). Log in with PFC_EMAIL / PFC_PASSWORD,
then replay the existing create flow exactly as a person would:
  1. Go to https://prayforchange.org/create
  2. Enter the title in the first field, click Continue
  3. Enter the story text in the story textarea
  4. Click "Generate Prayer", wait for generation
  5. Upload the news image to the file input on the page
  6. Verify the correct image shows (screenshot); retry with another image URL
     from the same article if it's wrong
  7. Click "Looks Good" / proceed through to publish
  8. Capture the published URL and screenshot

FAILURE HANDLING
If any step fails (login expired, site error, no story found, image failure),
do NOT silently die. Email me at NOTIFY_EMAIL_TO with: a clear description of
what failed, the error, and the FULL drafted prayer (title + 3 paragraphs) plus
the source links, so I can post it manually at https://prayforchange.org/create.
Add basic retry (e.g. 2 attempts with a short backoff) on network/posting steps.

ACCEPTANCE TEST
Give me a "Run once now" entry point I can trigger manually so I can test the
whole pipeline end to end before the daily schedule takes over. On that test run
it should either publish a real prayer or email me the drafted one with a clear
reason it couldn't publish.
